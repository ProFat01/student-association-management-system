How to print both sides
  1. Select Print / Save as PDF. Page 1 is the front of the card and page 2 is the back.
  2. Set the scale to 100% (actual size) and turn off headers and footers, so the card prints at its true size of 85.6 × 54 mm.
  3. For double-sided printing, choose two-sided with portrait paper and flip on long edge. Both sides are placed in the same position on their pages, so they line up when the sheet is turned over.
  4. If your printer cannot print on both sides, print the two pages separately and attach them back to back.

Best results on mobile

Open this page in Chrome and use Share → Print → Save as PDF for a cleaner result than the in-page print button on some phones.
